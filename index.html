Three ways to search

No key yet? Hit Load demo above to try the interface with sample comments.

Most precise

Paste a video URL

Add a phrase, a commenter, or both. Great for finding an old comment on a specific upload.

Creator-wide

Scan a channel

Leave the video URL empty and enter @handle or a channel link. We scan recent uploads.

Best effort

Search YouTube

Leave both URL and channel empty. YouTube is searched for videos matching the phrase, then their comments are scanned.

Heads-up: the official YouTube API cannot search every comment on YouTube. A video URL or channel gives reliable results; the no-scope mode is a best-effort shortcut.
